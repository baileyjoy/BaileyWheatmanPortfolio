About Me

Hi, I'm Bailey! I'm a Human Systems Engineering student at Arizona State University with a concentration in UX design. I'm passionate about creating digital experiences that are intuitive, accessible, and centered around people's needs. I'm currently developing my skill in user experience design, web development, and human-centered problem solving.

My Interests

I'm interested in how thoughtful design can make everyday interactions easier and more enjoyable. I enjoy exploring the relationship between people and technology, learning new coding skills, and finding creative solutions to design challenges.

My Approach to Design

I believe that the best designs are those that prioritize the user's needs and experiences. I strive to create solutions that are not only functional but also delightful to use. My design process involves extensive research, prototyping, and testing to ensure that every element serves a purpose and contributes to a positive user experience.

Beyond the Screen

Outside of my studies, I enjoy reading, exploring creative hobbies, and finding inspiration in everyday experiences. I enjoy opportunities to learn something new, experiment with creative ideas, and bring different perspectives into the work I do.
